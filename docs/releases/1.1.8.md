# iDuo Launcher 1.1.8

iDuo Launcher 1.1.8 (version code 11) follows feedback from testers: fewer Home rows, widgets you resize freely, a dock and status ring that make room for each other, and a status ring that opens settings.

## Highlights

- **Fewer rows**: **Settings → Home → Grid layout** now goes down to one row of apps below the widget band. Apps that no longer fit move to further pages, and **Undo last layout change** brings them back. A grid that does not fill the page rests on its bottom, above the page dots.
- **Free widget sizes**: a long press opens a frame with a handle on each corner. Drag any of them; apps in the way move aside. Tapping a widget in the picker places it and opens its frame at once.
- **Smaller menus**: **Widget options** and **Add to Home** are small floating windows with icons, beside the widget or your finger. A widget's exact size folds away under **Exact size**. **Customize launcher** is now **Customize & settings**.
- **Status ring**: tap it for **Battery**, **Wi-Fi** and **Mobile network** settings.
- **Liquid glass**: search has the style too, small folders now bend the wallpaper like the dock and large folders, and choosing the style explains that the wallpaper has to be set again in iDuo for all its effects.

## Fixes

- With six or more dock apps, the dock no longer runs into the status ring: the ring moves up to make room.
- The dock height slider now moves a tall dock, with the status on the right too. A dock aligned with the app rows has no slider; turn off **Align dock with app rows** to use it.
- A short press on a clock or date widget no longer opens its menu, and holding a widget responds a little sooner.
- The resize frame and placement preview are no longer shifted down on screens with a camera cutout.

## Install

Install from Google Play, or download `iDuoLauncher-1.1.8-release.apk` from this release. It updates 1.1.7 installed from the same source; the Play version and the GitHub APK are signed differently and do not update each other.

## Privacy

Nothing changes: no account, ads, analytics or tracking. See the [privacy policy](../privacy-policy.md).

## Known limits

- Widget sizes are free, so a widget made smaller than its app expects may cut off its content.
- **Grid layout** counts app rows; the widget band above them stays.
- Tested on a Galaxy Z Fold 7 and on emulators, including at the Fold 8's sizes, but not on a Fold 8 itself.
- Android does not let apps read a wallpaper set elsewhere, so over one the Liquid glass stays clear and does not bend it.
- New texts were translated with machine help and have not yet been reviewed by native speakers. Corrections are welcome.
