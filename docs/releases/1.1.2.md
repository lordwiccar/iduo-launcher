# iDuo Launcher 1.1.2

Fourth release on Google Play (version code 5). It makes folders easier to fill, adds pictures to Google News, shows more in the status ring and keeps widgets from other apps in place.

## Highlights

- **Folders**: the folder menu's **Add apps** ticks apps to add from a list; **Choose apps** ticks all of the folder's apps at once, and apps left unticked return to Home.
- **Google News pictures**: each article shows its picture, looked up once and kept on the device.
- **Status ring**: the mobile network (5G, 4G) always shows among the signal dots above the dock; without Wi-Fi the middle shows airplane mode or the battery level. The ring turns green while charging and yellow in battery saver.
- **Dock**: a short line parts your own apps from the recently opened ones.

## Fixes

- Widgets from other apps come back by themselves after their app is updated. iDuo now remembers each widget's app: if Android lets a widget go, iDuo connects it again, or shows it by name with **Reconnect**.
- Turning Wi-Fi off removes its symbol from the status ring.

## Install

Install or update from Google Play, or download `iDuoLauncher-1.1.2-release.apk` from this release. It updates 1.1.1 installed from the same source; the Play version and the GitHub APK are signed differently and do not update each other.

## Privacy

Still no account, ads, analytics or tracking. To find a Google News article's picture, iDuo asks Google for the article's address and reads the beginning of the article's page on the publisher's site, as opening it would. See the [privacy policy](../privacy-policy.md).

## Known limits

- Some publishers block automatic reading, so their articles show no picture.
- A widget that needs its own setup, or one Android does not let iDuo bind silently, waits for a tap on **Reconnect**.
