# iDuo Launcher 1.1.6

iDuo Launcher's first public release on Google Play (version code 9). It lets app icons take a shape of your choice and go dark or tinted, always or only with iDuo's dark mode.

## Highlights

- **Icon shape**: **Settings → Wallpaper & appearance → Icon shape** cuts app icons to a **Square**, **Squircle** or **Circle**, on Home, in the dock, in folders and in All apps. It goes well with **Folder shape**, added in 1.1.5.
- **Icon style**: **Original** keeps icons as their makers drew them. **Dark** puts each app's picture on a dark background. **Tinted** draws it in one light color on dark, from the single-color icon apps provide on Android 13 and later; apps without one use Dark.
- **Only in dark mode**: on by default, so a Dark or Tinted style follows iDuo's color mode, including sunrise / sunset.

## Fixes

- Work apps' icons follow the chosen shape and keep their work badge.

## Install

Install from Google Play, or download `iDuoLauncher-1.1.6-release.apk` from this release. It updates 1.1.5 installed from the same source; the Play version and the GitHub APK are signed differently and do not update each other.

## Privacy

Nothing changes: no account, ads, analytics or tracking. See the [privacy policy](../privacy-policy.md).

## Known limits

- Icons from an icon pack, and older icons that are not adaptive, keep their own shape and colors.
- Some apps draw a light plate into their icon's picture, so they stay light in the Dark style.
