# iDuo Launcher 1.1.0

Second release on Google Play (version code 3). It redesigns Settings, adds search and screen locking from Home, and replaces Google Discover with Google News.

## Highlights

- **New Settings** over the whole screen, grouped into Customization, Controls and System & support. Each section shows its current setting, search finds any setting by name, and on the inner screen the list stays beside the open section. Home settings keep a live preview with the Cover or Inner screen choice.
- **Home search**: swipe up on Home to find apps as you type; the search key or the magnifier sends the phrase to Google.
- **Double tap to lock** empty Home space, through the optional Home gestures service, so fingerprint and face unlock keep working. Every Home gesture has its own switch.
- **Google News** on the left page, with 86 editions from around the world and your choice of sections, or your own RSS feeds.
- **About, Changelog and Send feedback** under Help & information.
- Smooth Settings animations and support for Android's predictive back gesture.

## Install

Install or update from Google Play, or download `iDuoLauncher-1.1.0-release.apk` from this release. It updates 1.0.0 installed from the same source; the Play version and the GitHub APK are signed differently and do not update each other.

## Privacy

No account, ads, analytics or tracking. Headlines come directly from Google News or your RSS sources. A search phrase leaves the phone only when you send it to Google. Send feedback opens a Google form in the browser. See the [privacy policy](../privacy-policy.md).

## Known limits

- The optional accessibility service is needed to open notifications and Quick Settings with a swipe down and to lock the screen with a double tap.
- Google News headlines carry no pictures; your own RSS feeds show pictures when they provide them.
- Icon packs, notification dots and nested folders are not supported.
