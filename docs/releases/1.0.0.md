# iDuo Launcher 1.0.0

First release of iDuo Launcher and its first version on Google Play (version code 1).

## Highlights

- **Right-side dock** with four to eight apps, and a **4 × 4, 4 × 5 or 4 × 6 Home grid** chosen on a scrolling wheel.
- **Folders**: drop one app onto another. Folders size to their content, page beyond 6 × 6 apps, open with an animation over a blurred Home, and can be renamed, moved or ungrouped.
- **All apps** as an alphabetical list or a paged grid, with local search.
- **Left page**: Google Discover on supported devices, or a built-in **RSS reader** with your own sources.
- **Wallpaper**: Home shows your Android wallpaper, including live wallpapers. A photo or the bundled iDuo dunes can be set on the Home screen, lock screen or both.
- **Languages**: English, Czech, Slovak, Polish and German.
- Android widgets with resizing, work-profile apps, layout backup and restore, light, dark and sunrise/sunset appearance.

## Install

Install from Google Play, or download `iDuoLauncher-1.0.0-release.apk` from this release. iDuo is a new app with its own package (`media.whitewhale.iduo`); it does not replace earlier Duo Launcher builds.

## Privacy

No account, ads, analytics or tracking. See the [privacy policy](../privacy-policy.md).

## Known limits

- Google Discover relies on the installed Google app and may change with Google or Android updates; the RSS reader is the alternative.
- The optional accessibility service is needed to open notifications and Quick Settings with a swipe down on Home.
- Icon packs, notification dots and nested folders are not supported.
