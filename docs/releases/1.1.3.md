# iDuo Launcher 1.1.3

Fifth release on Google Play (version code 6). It adds a folding animation, shows a second SIM's signal, brings 5G and 4G back to the middle of the status ring, rounds menus and dialogs, and keeps Home from stopping between two pages.

## Highlights

- **Folding animation**: as you fold or unfold the phone, the half that swings tilts away from you, blurs and darkens toward its free edge, and Home comes into focus on the other screen. On the inner screen the half with the dock stays sharp. **Settings → Home → Screen → Folding animation** switches it off. It needs Android 13 or later.
- **Second SIM**: with two SIMs in use, the second SIM's signal shows as a second row of dots below the status ring above the dock and in the cover screen's top corner (Android 14 and later).
- **Status ring**: without Wi-Fi, the mobile network (5G, 4G) is back in the ring's middle, with four signal dots below.
- **Look**: menus, text fields and dialogs have rounded corners and iDuo's blue-grey colours instead of Material's lilac.
- **News page**: on the inner screen it takes one half, beside Home 1. Pages sliding out to the left fade away instead of vanishing at the end of the swipe.

## Fixes

- Home no longer stays stuck between two pages when the phone is folded, unfolded or rotated while a swipe is still settling.
- Renaming a folder puts the cursor after its name.

## Install

Install or update from Google Play, or download `iDuoLauncher-1.1.3-release.apk` from this release. It updates 1.1.2 installed from the same source; the Play version and the GitHub APK are signed differently and do not update each other.

## Privacy

Unchanged: no account, ads, analytics or tracking. See the [privacy policy](../privacy-policy.md).

## Known limits

- The animation starts once Android has switched screens: the inner screen lights up only when the phone is about half open, and folding on Home puts the phone to sleep, so the cover's part plays after you unlock it. Android's own wallpaper is blurred behind Home on Samsung phones only.
- The second SIM's row needs Android 14 or later; on Android 12 and 13 the ring shows one SIM's signal.
