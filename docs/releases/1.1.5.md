# iDuo Launcher 1.1.5

Seventh release on Google Play (version code 8). It adds large folders and a choice of folder shapes, draws the status ring's Wi-Fi symbol more lightly and keeps Home in place when the phone folds, rotates or changes display size.

## Highlights

- **Large folders**: open a folder on Home and choose **Make folder large** in its menu (⋮). It takes two by two places, like a folder in the App Library on iPhone: its first three apps are large icons that open with one tap, and the small icons in the last corner open the whole folder. It grows where it is or at the nearest free space on its page; if the page is full, the apps in its way move to the first free places. Drag it by any part to move it whole; **Make folder small** returns it to one place.
- **Folder shape**: **Settings → Wallpaper & appearance → Folder shape** draws folders on Home and in the dock as a **Square**, **Squircle** or **Circle**.
- **Status ring**: the Wi-Fi symbol has thinner lines.

## Fixes

- Folding, unfolding or rotating the phone, or changing the display size, no longer restarts Home. All apps no longer stays cut off between Home and the library, as reported on the Galaxy Z Fold 8's cover screen, and Home settles on a whole page even when a swipe's settle is interrupted.

## Install

Install or update from Google Play, or download `iDuoLauncher-1.1.5-release.apk` from this release. It updates 1.1.4 installed from the same source; the Play version and the GitHub APK are signed differently and do not update each other.

## Privacy

Nothing changes: no account, ads, analytics or tracking. See the [privacy policy](../privacy-policy.md).

## Known limits

- A large folder needs two free columns and rows of Home; in the dock it is always small.
