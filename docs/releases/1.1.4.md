# iDuo Launcher 1.1.4

Sixth release on Google Play (version code 7). It adds notification badges, a quicker way to put apps on Home and a letter index in All apps, refines the status ring and keeps the Galaxy Z Fold 8's cover in its own layout.

## Highlights

- **Notification badges**: an optional red badge on apps on Home, in the dock and inside folders counts their waiting notifications; a folder shows the total and a red dot on each of its small icons with notifications. **Settings → Home → Notification badges** turns it on and opens Android's notification access for iDuo.
- **Add apps**: long-press empty space on Home and choose **Add apps** to tick any number of apps not yet on Home. They fill the page from the place you held, then new pages after the last; the list shows how many pages that adds, such as **+1 page**.
- **All apps list**: its letters run down the right edge; touch or slide along them to jump to a letter.
- **Status ring**: the battery level always sits in the top opening, and without Wi-Fi or mobile data the middle shows a cell tower. With two SIMs, the first SIM's dots keep their usual place and the second SIM's signal is a smaller curve of dots inside the ring.
- **New Home pages**: dragging an app past the last page waits a little over a second at the right edge before opening an empty page, so pages are not added by accident.

## Fixes

- On a Galaxy Z Fold 8 cover set to a smaller display size, Home keeps the cover's one-page layout instead of the inner screen's two panes, with the library no longer cut off behind the dock, and the folding animation no longer stays frosted.
- Turning Wi-Fi off with no other network removes its symbol from the status ring at once.

## Install

Install or update from Google Play, or download `iDuoLauncher-1.1.4-release.apk` from this release. It updates 1.1.3 installed from the same source; the Play version and the GitHub APK are signed differently and do not update each other.

## Privacy

Notification badges are optional and need notification access. iDuo only counts each app's notifications: it does not read, keep or show their content, holds the counts in memory and sends nothing anywhere. Still no account, ads, analytics or tracking. See the [privacy policy](../privacy-policy.md).

## Known limits

- Ongoing notifications, such as music or a running timer, and apps whose badges are off in Android's settings do not count.
- The second SIM's dots need Android 14 or later.
